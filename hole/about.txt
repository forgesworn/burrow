What this is
============

You are reading a gopherhole that has no host.

Every document here is a signed Nostr event of kind 31436, named for
RFC 1436, the gopher protocol. The path you asked for is the event's
`d` tag. The signature is mine. The copies live on relays I chose,
and any bridge that can retrieve one can serve it to you, whether or
not the machine I published from is switched on.

That is the whole idea. Gopherspace has one endemic disease: holes
die with their hostname, and nothing in the protocol can prove who
wrote what. Nostr has identity as a keypair and documents as signed
events, but its clients are timelines that bury long writing an hour
after it is posted. Each one has what the other lacks.

gopherkind is the bridge between them. It is:

  a publisher      a directory of text files becomes signed events
  a bridge daemon  gopher, Gemini and HTTP from those same events
  a client         terminal browsing of gopherspace and Nostr alike

It is not custodial. It never holds a secret key, never generates
one, and never accepts an nsec. Writing goes through a NIP-46 remote
signer or a NIP-07 browser extension that stays yours.

Every npub is already a hole, published or not: profile, notes,
replies, threads, long-form articles, follows and followers are
generated from ordinary Nostr events. Authored documents like this
one simply shadow the generated paths. Taking over your own hole is
just publishing.
