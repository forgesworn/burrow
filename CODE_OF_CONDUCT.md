# Code of conduct

gopherkind is a small project with a small community, so this is short.

## What is expected

Be civil. Assume the person you are replying to is acting in good faith and is
short on time. Criticise the code, the specification or the argument, never the
person. Technical disagreement is welcome and is most of what happens here;
contempt is not.

Some specifics, because they come up in this corner of the world:

- People choose their own pronouns and their own names. Use them.
- Nostr is a public network. Do not post someone else's keys, real identity,
  location or private messages, and do not use an issue tracker to pressure a
  person by exposing them.
- No harassment, sexualised language, or sustained disruption. No advertising,
  token promotion or airdrop spam anywhere in this repository.
- Disagreements about scope end with the maintainer's decision. Forking is a
  legitimate answer to that; abuse is not.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages and any
space where you are representing the project. It does not extend to how you
behave elsewhere on Nostr, unless what you do there is aimed at someone in this
project.

time, not an incident desk.
